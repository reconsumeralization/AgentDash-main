# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors, users, and community members. We pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful and inclusive**: Use welcoming and inclusive language
- **Being collaborative**: Work together toward common goals
- **Being constructive**: Provide helpful feedback and suggestions
- **Being patient**: Understand that everyone has different experience levels
- **Being empathetic**: Consider the impact of your words and actions
- **Being open to learning**: Accept feedback and learn from mistakes

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment**: Use of sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- **Trolling**: Deliberate disruption of discussions or community activities
- **Insulting comments**: Personal attacks, derogatory comments, or inflammatory remarks
- **Public or private harassment**: Harassment of any kind, including in private messages
- **Publishing private information**: Sharing someone else's private information without permission
- **Inappropriate conduct**: Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it:

- **Email**: conduct@agentdash.com
- **GitHub Issues**: Create a private issue with the "conduct" label
- **Community Forum**: Use the private messaging system
- **Discord**: Contact a moderator directly

### Investigation Process

1. **Report Receipt**: All reports will be acknowledged within 24 hours
2. **Investigation**: Reports will be investigated by the project maintainers
3. **Resolution**: Appropriate action will be taken based on the investigation
4. **Follow-up**: Follow-up communication will be provided to all parties involved

### Enforcement Actions

Project maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including:

- **Warning**: A private or public warning to the individual
- **Temporary Ban**: A temporary ban from community spaces
- **Permanent Ban**: A permanent ban from community spaces
- **Project Ban**: Removal from project participation

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- **Project spaces**: All project repositories, issue trackers, and discussion forums
- **Public spaces**: When representing the project in public venues
- **Community spaces**: Discord servers, forums, and other community areas
- **Private communications**: When communicating about project-related matters
