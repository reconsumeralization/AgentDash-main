# Enhanced Priority Framework for Multi-Domain Objectives
## Impact, Feasibility, Dependencies, and Resource Requirements Analysis

## 🎯 Executive Summary

This enhanced framework provides a comprehensive system for categorizing and prioritizing your complex multi-domain objectives, incorporating impact assessment, feasibility analysis, dependency mapping, resource requirements, and risk mitigation strategies.

## 📊 Multi-Dimensional Priority Matrix

### Core Dimensions

#### 1. **Impact Assessment (0-100)**
- **Social Impact**: Benefit to society and democratic processes
- **Economic Impact**: Revenue potential and market disruption
- **Technological Impact**: Innovation and advancement potential
- **Strategic Impact**: Alignment with long-term vision

#### 2. **Feasibility Analysis (0-100)**
- **Technical Feasibility**: Current technology and expertise availability
- **Resource Feasibility**: Budget, time, and human resource requirements
- **Market Feasibility**: Market readiness and competitive landscape
- **Regulatory Feasibility**: Legal and compliance requirements

#### 3. **Dependency Mapping (0-100)**
- **Internal Dependencies**: Dependencies on other internal projects
- **External Dependencies**: Dependencies on external factors
- **Resource Dependencies**: Dependencies on specific resources
- **Timeline Dependencies**: Critical path and timing requirements

#### 4. **Resource Requirements (0-100)**
- **Development Resources**: Technical development needs
- **Operational Resources**: Ongoing operational requirements
- **Financial Resources**: Budget and funding needs
- **Human Resources**: Team and expertise requirements
